# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, GSAP, ScrollTrigger, Lenis

## Users

Founders, business owners, creative directors, and marketing leaders looking for high-calibre digital presence, bespoke design craft, and reliable turnaround without agency overhead.

## Product Purpose

Boutique portfolio and direct-inquiry platform for Juan Pablo Chacón (JP Studios). Showcases world-class creative web development, tactile fluid micro-interactions, and editorial typography, converting qualified leads into client projects.

## Positioning

Bespoke web design and production crafted in pure React 19—zero generic page builders, zero AI slop templates. Transparent 14-day delivery schedule with signed milestones (48h strategy, 10d live mobile preview, 14d turnkey deployment) and direct founder-level communication.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile modern web browsers. Evaluated by busy business leaders who scan for credibility in under 10 seconds, expect instant load speeds, tactile interaction feedback, and low-friction direct outreach via WhatsApp and business email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- High-performance client-side rendering with sub-second asset delivery.
- Symmetrical i18n support in Spanish (`es`) and English (`en`).
- Dual contact channel routing (WhatsApp and corporate email) via Web3Forms.
- Interactive showcase and case studies with real interactive simulations (e.g. SAI).
- Adherence to editorial typography standards: natural sentence casing, lightweight display weights, zero decorative slash-labels.
- Pure solid graphite surfaces (`#1C1D20`) with zero fake noise or grain overlays.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** JP Studios / Juan Pablo Chacón
- **Tone:** Grounded, candid, pragmatic, senior craftsperson.
- **Visual Stance:** Restrained luxury, editorial typography, tactile physical micro-interactions, pure monochrome surfaces.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live interactive prototype and case study for SAI (hotel/club booking experience).
- Production-grade codebase passing all TypeScript and Vite build verifications.

## Product Principles

1. **Craft Over Convenience:** Bespoke code tailored to the client's business rather than generic visual templates.
2. **Speed with Certainty:** A strict, transparent 14-day timeline with predictable deliverables at each phase.
3. **Founder Accountability:** Direct collaboration with the craftsperson building the site, eliminating agency layers and communication drag.
4. **Honest Conversion:** Low-friction contact routing (WhatsApp/Email) with guaranteed same-business-day response.
